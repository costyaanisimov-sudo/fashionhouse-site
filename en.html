Our Role

Fashion House Group acts as full-cycle developer, operator and asset manager. We handle master planning, architectural concept, leasing and day-to-day operations — from the first sketch to the opening day and beyond.

Our model gives investors and partners transparent, measurable results at every stage — from market analysis to real-time retail analytics.

Operating Model

Our leasing team builds tenant mixes focused on international fashion brands and Russian designers. Deep knowledge of the outlet market allows us to attract anchor tenants and deliver consistent footfall.

All assets are managed through our proprietary retail analytics platform, tracking conversion rates, average basket size and shopper behaviour in real time.

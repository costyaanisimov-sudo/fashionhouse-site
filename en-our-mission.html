Fashion House Group

OUR MISSION

"We build effective business and long-term partnerships"

We build effective business and long-term partnerships and create conditions that help our tenants successfully develop their business in outlet centres.

Our team is focused on a high level of service and close interaction with brands. We help tenants improve business performance and together we create a quality and comfortable shopping experience for visitors.

The success of our tenants is the foundation of the success of each outlet centre. That is why our goal is to create sustainable value for brands, owners, investors and other partners of Fashion House Group.

WE KNOW THE OUTLET BUSINESS FROM THE INSIDE

Fashion House Group has practical expertise in creating and managing outlet centres — from the initial idea and project potential assessment to the opening of the facility and its subsequent operational development.

We work with investors, developers and commercial real estate owners and can engage at individual stages of a project or provide comprehensive support throughout the entire lifecycle of the facility.

01

DEVELOPMENT & CONSULTING

We help define the potential of a future outlet project, shape its concept and develop an optimal development model. Fashion House Group's expertise includes market and catchment area analysis, project positioning, concept development, master plan and architectural recommendations, merchandise and brand strategy, and pre-opening support.

02

OUTLET CENTRE MANAGEMENT

We take on comprehensive management of operating outlet centres and help owners increase operational efficiency and long-term asset value. Our management model combines commercial management, tenant relations, operations, marketing, performance analytics and customer experience development.

03

BROKERAGE & LEASING

We create a balanced tenant mix that reflects the positioning of the property, its target audience and commercial objectives. We manage the entire process — from developing a leasing strategy and identifying potential tenants to brand negotiations, lease execution and ongoing tenant relations. Fashion House Group's longstanding relationships with Russian and international fashion brands give us a deep understanding of operator requirements and the specifics of brand operations in the outlet format.

04

MARKETING

We develop marketing strategies aimed not only at brand awareness, but primarily at growing footfall, tenant sales and the commercial performance of the outlet centre. We combine strategic positioning, media planning, advertising campaigns, events, digital communications and analytics, adapting tools to each market and the objectives of each project.
